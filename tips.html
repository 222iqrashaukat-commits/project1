PLANT CARE

Simple Care Tips

Follow these simple tips to keep your plants healthy.

01
💧

Water Properly

Check the soil before watering. Avoid giving too much water to your plant.

02
☀️

Give Enough Sunlight

Place your plant where it can receive the amount of sunlight it needs.

03
✂️

Remove Dry Leaves

Remove dead or dry leaves to keep the plant clean and healthy.

04
🪴

Use Good Soil

Healthy soil helps plants receive nutrients and grow properly.

05
🌬️

Keep Air Moving

Good air circulation can help create a healthy environment for plants.

06
🔍

Check Your Plants

Regularly check your plants for dry leaves or other visible problems.

🌱 Tip of the Day

Remember to check the soil before watering.
